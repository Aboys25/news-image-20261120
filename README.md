# 20 November 2026 — The World in One Page

A newspaper front-page poster summing up the world news of
**Friday, 20 November 2026**. `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library
only, no network) decodes `entry.png.b64` (the poster as base64 text,
so the repository stays text-only) back to `entry.png` in the output
directory.

## The stories

1. **Cyclone Akvan bears down on Odisha, India** — Severe Cyclonic
   Storm Akvan was due to cross the Andhra Pradesh–Odisha coast near
   Gopalpur around noon on Friday 20 November, with sustained winds of
   90–100 km/h gusting to 110 km/h. About 300,000 people had been
   evacuated to shelters and 423 rescue teams deployed.
   Sources: The Hindu, “Cyclone ‘Akvan’ updates: Three lakh evacuated,
   423 rescue teams deployed as cyclone heads to Odisha”, 20 Nov 2026;
   India Meteorological Department bulletin, 19 Nov 2026
   (https://www.thehindu.com, https://mausam.imd.gov.in).
2. **Trump: no Iran decision until 2027** — US President Donald Trump,
   returning from China, told Reuters, Axios and Fox News on Thursday
   that he was in no rush on renewed strikes against Iran: “We’ll see
   what happens after January 1.” The remarks led the 20 November
   morning editions worldwide.
   Sources: Reuters via SRN News, “Trump eyes 2027 to ‘see what
   happens’ with Iran”, 19 Nov 2026
   (https://srnnews.com/trump-eyes-2027-to-see-what-happens-with-iran/);
   New York Post, 19 Nov 2026.
3. **Europe: ready for talks with Russia on Ukraine** — At the Berlin
   summit of the Coalition of the Willing, European leaders said they
   were ready to negotiate with Russia on ending the war in Ukraine,
   with the UK's Andy Burnham citing a “clear and consistent and
   united negotiating position” and Germany's Friedrich Merz calling
   Moscow's demands unrealistic.
   Source: BBC News, “Europe ready for talks with Russia on ending war
   in Ukraine, leaders say”, 19 Nov 2026 (reported in 20 Nov editions).
4. **GTA VI: $1.84bn in one day** — Grand Theft Auto VI launched on
   19 November and, per Sensor Tower estimates, generated about
   $1.84 billion from ~19 million copies by launch day — passing the
   entire $1.805bn global box-office run of Christopher Nolan's
   *The Odyssey*.
   Sources: 80.lv, “Report: GTA 6 Generated Nearly $2B in Sales by
   Launch Day”, 19 Nov 2026; Forbes, 19 Nov 2026.
5. **Also today: World Children's Day** — 20 November is the UN's
   World Children's Day.

## Verification notes

Stories deliberately excluded after date checks: the ICJ ruling that
Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya (delivered Thursday
19 November), the Russian strikes that damaged the Austrian and Dutch
embassies in Kyiv (19 November), the Houthi strike on Riyadh's PIF
Tower (19 November), NASA's Voyager 1 one-light-day confirmation
(19 November), and JAXA's Hayabusa2 retargeting (announced
17 November). Every story in the image either happened on
20 November 2026 or was first reported in the 20 November editions
(in Asia-Pacific time zones).
